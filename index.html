Published paper

Whole-genome analysis of Brevibacterium sanguinis AZMABM HM27: a bacterial isolate from the sea anemone Radianthus magnifica and exhibiting promising multi-therapeutic properties.

Molecular Biology Reports (Springer Nature), 2026, 53:1513 · Published online 3 September 2026 · I am the 3rd of 11 authors · doi.org/10.1007/s11033-026-12717-7

The team isolated a bacterium from a sea anemone collected at Samalona Island, South Sulawesi. They sequenced its genome and tested its extract for bioactivity. To the authors’ knowledge, this is the first report of this species as a marine symbiont.

97.37%OrthoANI with B. sanguinis type strain (dDDH 76.50%)
3.83 Mbpone chromosome, GC 68.3%, 3,362 protein-coding genes
5predicted biosynthetic gene clusters (ectoine, terpene, siderophore and others)
41compounds detected by GC-MS

The crude extract showed antioxidant, anti-inflammatory and antidiabetic activity in vitro, and dose-dependent antiproliferative activity on MCF-7 cells (74.10% viability at 1 mg/mL). The authors state that purification and in vivo studies are still needed.

Undergraduate thesis

Screening anti-quorum sensing activity of marine sponge-associated yeast from Raja Ampat against Pseudomonas aeruginosa · BRIN, Jan – Aug 2025

  • Isolated yeast from marine sponges on YM and YPD agar and purified six isolates
  • Extracted DNA, amplified 18S rRNA by PCR, ran gel electrophoresis, and identified isolates with NCBI BLAST
  • Tested extracts at three concentrations in triplicate with an agar well diffusion pyocyanin assay
  • Analyzed the data with ANOVA in GraphPad Prism and SPSS
  • Ran molecular docking on my own with AutoDock Vina from the command line, preparing ligands from PubChem and receptors from RCSB PDB
  • Two isolates, YSP9.3 and YSP13.2, reduced pyocyanin pigment without stopping bacterial growth, which points to anti-quorum sensing activity
  • Docking suggested that diketopiperazine-like compounds from GC-MS bind well to virulence proteins of P. aeruginosa. They are possible candidates and need further testing.

Supervisors: Dr. Santi Rukminita Anggraeni and Dr. Apon Zaenal Mustopa.
